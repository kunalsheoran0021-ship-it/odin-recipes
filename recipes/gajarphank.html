Gajarphank

Ingredients

  1. 1 kg fresh carrots
  2. 1 litre full-fat milk
  3. ½ cup sugar
  4. 4 tablespoons ghee
  5. ¼ teaspoon cardamom powder
  6. 10–12 almonds
  7. 10–12 cashews
  8. 2 tablespoons raisins
  9. 2 tablespoons khoya (optional)

Steps

  1. Wash and peel the carrots.
  2. Grate all the carrots using a grater.
  3. Heat ghee in a large pan.
  4. Add the grated carrots and cook for 5–7 minutes.
  5. Add milk to the carrots.
  6. Cook on medium heat, stirring occasionally.
  7. Continue cooking until the milk reduces and the carrots become soft.
  8. Add sugar and mix well.
  9. Add cardamom powder.
  10. Add chopped almonds, cashews and raisins.
  11. Add khoya if desired.
  12. Cook for another 5–10 minutes until the halwa becomes thick.
  13. Garnish with additional dry fruits.
  14. Serve warm and enjoy your homemade Gajar Ka Halwa!
