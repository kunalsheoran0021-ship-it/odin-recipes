Gulabjamun

Ingredients

  1. 1 cup milk powder
  2. 1/4 cup all-purpose flour (maida)
  3. 2 tablespoons ghee
  4. 1/4 teaspoon baking soda
  5. Milk as needed
  6. Oil or ghee for frying

Steps

  1. Take milk powder, all-purpose flour and baking soda in a bowl.
  2. Add ghee and mix everything well.
  3. Add milk little by little and make a soft dough.
  4. Let the dough rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Make small smooth balls from the dough without cracks.
  6. Take a pan and add sugar and water.
  7. Heat until the sugar dissolves completely.
  8. Add cardamom pods and saffron strands.
  9. Cook the syrup for a few minutes until slightly sticky.
  10. Heat oil or ghee on low to medium heat.
  11. Carefully fry the balls until golden brown, turning gently.
  12. Remove the gulab jamuns and let excess oil drain.
  13. Place the warm gulab jamuns into warm sugar syrup.
  14. Let them soak for 1–2 hours.
  15. Add rose water and serve warm.
